# Cross-Level Information Processing: Research Project Outline

## Document Structure

### Document 1: Theoretical Foundation
**"Cross-Level Information Processing in Complex Systems: From Bell's Neuroscience to AI Architectures"**

- **Core Theory**: Bell's emergence/submergence framework and cross-level dynamics
- **Biological Evidence**: Networks of networks structure in neural systems
- **Generalization**: Extension beyond neuroscience to general systems theory
- **AI Connections**: Initial identification of similar principles in transformer architectures
- **Conceptual Bridge**: How these principles might constitute fundamental "laws" of complex information processing

### Document 2: Phase I Study
**"Identifying Cross-Level Dynamics in Current Transformer Architectures"**

- **Systematic Analysis**: Complete lifecycle examination (training, inference, infrastructure, interaction)
- **Principle Mapping**: Where emergence/submergence operations occur in current LLMs
- **Evidence Gathering**: Behavioral and architectural indicators of cross-level processing
- **Gap Analysis**: Where current architectures fall short of Bell's framework
- **Validation Methods**: How to test for presence/absence of these principles

### Document 3: Phase II Study
**"Multi-Transformer Cross-Level Systems: Design and Implementation"**

- **Middleware Architecture**: Using existing protocols (MCP, A2A, ACP) as emergence/submergence operations
- **Multi-Model Orchestration**: Different model sizes operating at different levels/timescales
- **System Design**: How to create stable cross-level information flows between models
- **Implementation Strategy**: Practical approaches using available infrastructure
- **Success Metrics**: How to evaluate whether the system exhibits genuine cross-level dynamics
